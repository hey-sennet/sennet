# Code of conduct

Sennet is for everyone who wants to talk to their coding agents, and so is its community.
Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else the project
gathers, is expected to help keep it welcoming.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new, or new to English.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Criticise ideas and code, not people. Say what would make a change better.
- Accept a "no" on a proposal gracefully; it's about the project's direction, not you.
- Respect people's time, including the maintainers', who are volunteers.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats, or demeaning comments about anyone's identity,
  background, body or ability.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberate derailing, or continuing after being asked to stop.

## What happens

The maintainers will look into every report and may respond with a private word, a
public warning, removal of comments, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's
spaces, depending on what happened. They'll tell the person who reported it what was
done, where they can.

This applies in the project's own spaces, and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. It's inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
